SignBridge

AI-Powered ASL Accessibility & Learning Platform

Live Demo →  ·  GitHub →
Free hosting — first load may take ~30–50s to wake up.

A full-stack accessibility and learning platform for American Sign Language, built around three integrated modules — real-time computer-vision fingerspelling recognition, everyday sign communication, and a gamified learning curriculum — designed around how different learners and signers actually use ASL.

Computer VisionAccessibility GamificationReact FastAPI
Explore the three modules
Recognition

Webcam-based ASL fingerspelling recognition (A–Z) using MediaPipe hand-landmark detection and a scikit-learn MLP classifier trained on real image data — ~86% accuracy on a held-out test set (small sample; not yet validated across real-world webcam conditions).

↓
Interpretation

Types English text and plays back verified sign video for supported vocabulary, with fingerspelling as a fallback for everything else — a supported-vocabulary communication aid, not a full grammatical English-to-ASL translator.

↓
Learning

Gamified curriculum — XP, levels, streaks, hearts, and achievements — covering the full A–Z alphabet plus a verified vocabulary library, with real signer videos and source attribution, comprehension quizzes, and camera practice validated against the recognition model. Actively expanding with per-sign mastery tracking, quests, and contextual practice scenarios.

The recognition model is objectively validated on A–Z fingerspelling only; vocabulary-sign practice in Learning is self-guided and self-assessed, not model-graded.
